## Post-Submission

### Review Timeline

**Typical Timeline:**
- **Submitted:** Day 0 (today)
- **In Review:** 1-3 days
- **Approved:** 1-3 days (average 24-48 hours)
- **Total:** 2-6 days

**Check status:** App Store Connect → Apps → Findr → iOS App → Activity

---

### Possible Outcomes

#### 1. Approved ✅
**Status:** "Ready for Sale"
**Next Steps:**
- App goes live automatically (if auto-release selected)
- Appears in App Store within 24 hours
- Monitor downloads in App Store Connect Analytics
- Watch for user reviews
- Monitor crash reports in Sentry

#### 2. Rejected ❌
**Status:** "Rejected"
**Common Rejection Reasons:**
- Missing permissions in Info.plist
- Privacy policy doesn't match app behavior
- Demo account doesn't work
- App crashes on launch
- Guideline violation (spam, content, etc.)

**Next Steps:**
- Read rejection reason carefully
- Fix issues
- Respond in Resolution Center
- Re-submit (usually faster review 2nd time)

#### 3. Metadata Rejected ⚠️
**Status:** "Metadata Rejected"
**Reason:** Issue with description, screenshots, or metadata (not the app itself)

**Next Steps:**
- Fix metadata issue
- No new build needed
- Re-submit immediately

#### 4. Developer Rejected
**If you need to reject your own submission:**
- App Store Connect → Version → "Developer Rejected"
- Fix issues
- Re-submit when ready

---

### Communication with Apple
