🧹 Clear Service Worker Cache

⚠️ Cache Issue Detected
Your browser is serving old cached iNaturalist URLs. This tool will help clear the cache.

Automatic Fix

Manual Steps (if automatic fails)

Step 1: Open DevTools (F12 or Cmd+Option+I)
Step 2: Go to Application tab (Chrome/Edge) or Storage tab (Firefox)
Step 3: Click Service Workers in left sidebar
Click Unregister next to any fishfindr.eu workers
Step 4: Click Cache Storage in left sidebar
Right-click each cache → Delete
Step 5: Close DevTools and do a hard refresh (Cmd+Shift+R or Ctrl+Shift+F5)

Verify the Fix
