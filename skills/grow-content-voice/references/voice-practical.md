# Voice Mode — Practical / Task Copy

**Read this when:** writing in-app task descriptions, HowTo step content (the steps themselves; the framing can be editorial), push notification copy, onboarding strings, empty states, error messages, the "what to do this week" panel.

## Touchstone

**Charles Dowding's** task-writing voice in his books and on his blog — direct, observational, imperative, every sentence does work.

## Cadence

Short sentences. Imperative verbs. Numbers and dates over vague time references. No hedging unless the hedge is load-bearing.

## Structure for a HowTo step

Verb + object + timing/condition + (optional) why.

## Examples — task descriptions

> Sow tomato seed indoors. 18–22°C in a propagator or warm windowsill. They'll germinate in 7–10 days.

> Pinch out side-shoots on cordon tomatoes. Once a week from late June. Leaves the plant's energy for the fruit.

> Earth up potatoes. Draw soil up the stems when shoots reach 20 cm. Repeat every fortnight until the foliage closes the row.

> Net the brassicas. The cabbage whites are flying — they lay on anything in the family.

## Example — push notification

> Frost warning tonight in your area. Cover the courgettes and the dahlia tubers if they're out.

## Example — empty state for "My Garden"

> Add your first plant to get task suggestions, planting windows, and the year-at-a-glance view. Start with something you grow — or want to.

## Example — error state for failed location fetch

> Couldn't get your location. Tap to set it manually — we need it for frost dates and timing.

## What practical voice avoids

- **"Simply"** — patronising filler. ("Simply pinch out the side-shoots" — no, just say "pinch out the side-shoots".)
- **"Just"** used the same way.
- **"You'll want to"** — say what to do.
- **"It is recommended that…"** — passive evasion.
- Cheerful exclamation marks (more than one per page is too many).
- Emoji except in onboarding hero moments and the empty-state "no work to do" placeholder ("Chill, no work to do 🌿" is OK once; don't stack them).

## Word budgets

- 5-word app string: ruthless. ("Sow runner beans this week.")
- 30-word task description: imperative, with one piece of why. ("Sow runner beans direct, 5 cm deep, in a sunny spot. Provide canes or netting before they reach 30 cm — they grow fast once they go.")
- 80-word HowTo step: a verb-led paragraph with timing and condition. Don't expand into editorial.

## Anti-smugness in practical voice

Practical voice is the easiest place for smugness to creep in via diminutive language ("simply", "just"). See `anti-smugness.md` for the full check, but the practical-voice version is: if you removed the word, would the sentence still be a complete instruction? If yes, the word was filler.
