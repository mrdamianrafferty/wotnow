# Voice Mode — Quick-Answer SEO Lead

**Read this when:** writing the 80–150 word paragraph that opens every plant page; FAQ answers; schema content (HowTo and FAQPage JSON-LD `description` fields); content that AI assistants will quote.

## Why this voice exists separately

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Overviews extract direct answers from the top of pages. The paragraph above the fold is the paragraph that gets cited. It has to:

1. Carry a number in the first sentence
2. Give a direct answer in the first 30 words
3. Be UK-specific enough that the AI won't paraphrase it into something generic

## Structure

1. **Sentence 1:** the direct answer with a number. "Sow tomato seed indoors from late February to early April." Not "Tomatoes need careful timing." Not "Many factors affect when to sow tomatoes."
2. **Sentence 2–3:** the region nuance. "Earlier in Cornwall, later in Aberdeen — south-coast gardeners can start in mid-February, upland Scotland in early April."
3. **Sentence 4–6:** the next-most-important fact (planting out, harvest window, RHS hardiness, sun/soil requirement).
4. **Sentence 7–8 (if needed):** a caveat that makes the advice trustworthy. "Tomatoes are frost-tender (RHS H1c) — wait until the last frost has passed before planting outdoors."

## Word target: 80–150 words

Shorter than that and there isn't enough for the AI to extract. Longer and the cited fragment will be too dilute.

## Example — quick-answer lead for `/grow/species/tomato`

> Sow tomato seed indoors from late February to early April for greenhouse growing, or from mid-March to mid-April for outdoor crops. South-coast gardeners can start a fortnight earlier; upland Scotland a fortnight later. Plant out once the last frost has passed — typically mid-May in southern England, early June in the north. Tomatoes are frost-tender (RHS H1c) and won't tolerate even a light frost on the foliage. They need a sunny, sheltered spot with rich, well-drained soil. Outdoor crops produce fruit from July to October; greenhouse and polytunnel crops crop earlier and for longer. Cordon (indeterminate) varieties give the longest pick; bush (determinate) varieties are best for outdoor growing in shorter seasons.

That's 137 words. Number in sentence one. UK-specific throughout. RHS rating where USDA would be in an American piece. Region nuance. A factual hierarchy that AI extractors can chunk cleanly.

## Example — FAQ answer

> **Q: When should I plant out my tomatoes in the UK?**
>
> Wait until after the last frost — typically mid-May in southern England, late May in the Midlands, early June in northern England and Scotland. Harden tomatoes off for 7–10 days before planting out, exposing them to outdoor conditions for longer periods each day. Plant 60 cm apart for bush varieties, 45 cm for cordons. Water in well, and provide a stake or cane for cordons at planting time.

105 words. Direct answer in the first sentence with a date. UK-specific. Practical follow-on.

## What this voice avoids

- Sentences without numbers in the first paragraph
- Generic verbs at the start ("Tomatoes are a popular crop…" — get to the answer)
- Anything that could have been written about any country
- US frost-date frames ("after the last freeze")
- USDA zones (use RHS H-ratings — see `grow-gardening-expert` references)
- "Best practices" phrasing — directly state the practice instead

## When quick-answer voice ends

The quick-answer paragraph is the first 80–150 words. After that, the page moves into editorial voice (for long-form sections) or practical voice (for HowTo steps, FAQ pairs). Don't carry quick-answer compression through the whole page — it reads as relentless after three paragraphs.
